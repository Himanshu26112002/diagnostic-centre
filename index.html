Frequently Asked Questions

Clear clinical answers regarding fasting, bladder fullness, and PC-PNDT regulations.

Fasting allows the gallbladder to remain distended with bile and reduces obscured bowel gas shadows. This allows the radiologist to evaluate your liver, gallstones, biliary ducts, and pancreas with optimal acoustic clarity. Plain water is permitted.
A full urinary bladder serves as an acoustic window, displacing air-filled bowel loops and allowing crisp visualization of pelvic structures, uterus, ovaries, or prostate. Drink 4 to 5 glasses of plain water 1 hour prior to your scan and avoid passing urine until examined.
Yes. Diagnostic sonography utilizes high-frequency acoustic waves without any ionizing radiation (unlike X-rays or CT scans). Over 45 years of clinical research confirm that it is entirely safe for developing fetuses when performed by licensed medical practitioners.
Printed HD gloss film and physician-signed reports are delivered within 30 to 45 minutes post-examination. Additionally, an encrypted PDF copy is instantly sent to your registered WhatsApp mobile number.
Yes, under the statutory PC-PNDT Act 1994, all ultrasound examinations strictly require a valid medical prescription signed by a registered doctor, together with an official government photo identity card (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport).
